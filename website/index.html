The Concept

A game built for those who live fashion

THE FASHION HOUSE™ is a premium strategy board game where players build a fashion empire — from sketching your first silhouette to dominating the global runway. Each game is a new season, and no two seasons are alike.

Move across 20 board spaces, draw from six distinct card decks, and outmanoeuvre rival houses. The first to reach 100 Prestige claims the runway — and the title of Fashion House Champion.

How to Play

Six acts.
One winner.

✦

Choose Your House

Select your role — Designer, Stylist, Buyer, or Entrepreneur. Each comes with a unique ability that shapes your strategy from the very first turn.

◎

Move the Board

Roll and land on 20 board spaces. From Inspiration and the Fabric Market to Celebrity Client and Award Ceremony — every space triggers an action.

◈

Build Collections

In the Design Studio, combine a silhouette with a fabric and style direction. The right pairing earns synergy bonuses no other player can replicate.

⟡

Draw Your Cards

Six decks — Inspiration, Fabric, Trend, Event, Celebrity, and Buyer — bring windfalls and setbacks. A magazine cover today; a fabric delay tomorrow.

★

Conquer Fashion Week

Land on Fashion Week and face the judges. Five scoring criteria determine your Prestige gain. Your biggest opportunity — and your greatest risk.

♔

Own the Runway

Open boutiques for passive income, attract celebrity endorsements, grow your Follower count. Reach 100 Prestige first — the runway is yours.

The Players

Choose
your role

Every Fashion House is built on a different kind of ambition. Pick the player whose instincts match yours — then use them ruthlessly.

✂

The Designer

+2 Creativity per Collection

A visionary who transforms raw inspiration into iconic pieces. Premium fabrics cost you less, and your collections score higher at Fashion Week.

◈

The Stylist

Trend Amplifier

When a Trend Card aligns with your style direction, you double the bonus. Every Fashion Shoot adds 500 Followers to your orbit automatically.

◎

The Buyer

Deal Closer

Commercial instinct made concrete. Gain a bonus on every Buyer Card, earn passive boutique income sooner, and start the game with one already open.

♛

The Entrepreneur

Brand Builder

You play the long game. Boutiques open at a discount, Celebrity Cards grant extra Prestige, and your brand starts with more recognition than anyone else.
