The Game

Everyone at the table is the entertainment

No screens to hide behind, no downtime between turns. Every card sends someone into the spotlight — and everyone else gets a vote on whether they nailed it.

🎴

Draw & Perform

Flip a Power Card. It sets your venue and your animal. Perform the next prompt from your own Prompt Card — jokes, songs, mime, or acting, in order, no cherry-picking.

🗳️

The Table Decides

Depending on the prompt, you're judged by majority vote, by whether the table can see it happen, or by a single named judge. No mercy, no re-dos.

🏆

Race to 2 Tokens

Match your Character's animal or venue to bank rewards fast, or hoard Power Cards for their chaos powers. First to 2 Prop Tokens takes a bow and wins.

Free Digital Companion

The app plays referee, so you don't have to

No rulebook flipping mid-performance. The Companion App tracks decks, hands, tokens and powers for you, and works on any phone or tablet at the table.

  • Auto-detects Animal Affinity and Venue Match — no manual scoring
  • Built-in Tutorial Mode walks new players through their first game
  • Family Mode removes adult prompts and two chaos cards instantly
  • Real-time multiplayer — pass devices, or everyone joins from their own
