The Game

Everyone at the table is the entertainment

No screens to hide behind, no downtime between turns. Every Power Card sends someone into the spotlight — and having a go is all it takes to succeed.

🎴

Draw & Perform

Flip a Power Card. Its venue decides what kind of performance you give, and your Prompt Card supplies the exact material.

🎭

Have a Go, You Succeed

Nobody scores your performance. Attempt it in good faith and it counts — the only way to fail is refusing. Can't sing? Pass and draw again, no penalty.

🏆

Collect Prop Tokens

Draw your own animal for an instant Prop Token, or hold two Power Cards with the same name and cash the pair. First to the target takes a bow.

The Deck

52 Power Cards, 13 of them different

Each of the thirteen Power Cards appears once in every venue. Hold two with the same name and you can cash the pair for a Prop Token — so the card you need is always sitting face-up in front of somebody.

🎨 A sample of the deck. Card artwork is being refreshed against the latest rules, so a few cards are not shown yet.

Pick Your Animal

8 Character Cards

Your Character Card fixes your animal — draw a Power Card carrying it and that's an instant Prop Token — and your home venue, which sets the kind of performance you give.

🎨 Artwork shown is from an earlier print run; the rules text on these cards is being updated.

The Goal

First to the Prop Token target wins

Eight Prop Tokens sit in the Pool at the centre of the table. Collect three at 3–5 players, or two at 6–8, and the game ends on the spot.

Free Digital Companion

Play it right now, in your browser

The Companion App is the digital table. It deals the Power Cards, tracks every hand and Prop Token, and calls Animal Affinity for you — no rulebook flipping mid-performance.

  • Deals and shuffles the 52 Power Cards for you
  • Spots your pairs and cashes them for Prop Tokens
  • Shows every player's Power Cards face-up, so you know what to target
  • Built-in tutorial for a table that has never played
  • Pass one phone around, or everyone joins from their own
