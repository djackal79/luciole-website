How to Play

Everything you need to run a full game at the table — or let the Companion App run it for you.

🎲 Let the App Guide You

🏆 The Goal

Be the first player to collect your table's target number of Prop Tokens. Bigger tables play to a lower target, so the game still finishes in a sensible time.

PlayersTokens to win
3–53 Prop Tokens
6–82 Prop Tokens
💡 The Companion App sets the target automatically from your player count — the number to beat is shown at the top of every screen.

🎭 The Four Venues

Every Power Card and every Character belongs to one venue. The venue tells you what kind of performance is required.

VenueWhat you do
🎤 Comedy LoungeTell jokes — setup, punchline, deadpan delivery
🎵 The ClubSing or rhyme — lyrics, rhythm, improvised verse
🎪 The Royal ShowClown around — mime, physical stunts, slapstick
🎭 The School PlayAct — accents, emotions, roleplay, impressions

🧩 Setup

Pick the number of players (3–8). Each player pulls a cracker and opens a packet of 5 cards: one Character Card and four Prompt Cards, one for each venue, with 8 prompts on each.

Your Character Card fixes your animal and your home venue. The packet is fixed — choosing your Character chooses your Prompt Cards too. Player 1 starts.

🎁 Each cracker opened also puts one Prop Token into the Pool, so the Pool starts with one token per player. You can open a spare cracker at any time to add another.

🔁 Your Turn

Power first, then cash, then draw. The draw is last — that ordering is deliberate.

1Play a power (optional, one card). Some cards have their own printed timing that overrides this step.

2Cash any pairs. Two Power Cards of the same name — discard both, take a Prop Token.

3Draw one Power Card, face-up for everyone to see. It shows a venue, an animal and a printed power.

🔒 A card can never be cashed on the turn it is drawn. Because the draw comes last, every card you take sits face-up in front of you through a full round of everyone else's turns before you can spend it. That is exactly what gives Stage Hook, Pie in the Face and Swoop their teeth.

Two exceptions: Animal Affinity pays out immediately on the draw, and a card you acquire rather than draw — pinched with Pie in the Face, or Swooped off the discard — can be cashed the same turn.

4Perform. The card's venue tells you which kind of performance to give. Use the next unused prompt for that venue from your Prompt Card. Prompts are used in order — no going back, no cherry-picking.

🎭 If you have a go, you succeed. Quality is irrelevant — nobody scores your joke. The only way to fail is to refuse, or to make no real attempt. The performer decides when they are done, ties go to the performer, and it takes a unanimous "you didn't even try" to fail someone.

Can't do this one? If you genuinely can't — you can't sing, you don't know the song — you may pass and draw again instead of taking a failure. That's a rule, not a favour.

5Resolve.

Refused → the card goes to the discard pile. Turn ends.

Attempt made → your reward:

  1. 🐾 Animal Affinity — Does the animal on the card match your Character's animal? If yes, take a Prop Token. Turn ends.
  2. 🃏 No animal match — Keep the card face-up in front of you. It counts toward a pair and its printed power is now available. Turn ends.
💡 Playing digitally? The Companion App auto-detects the outcome from the card data — no manual scoring needed.

🔁 Cashing a Pair

If you hold two Power Cards with the same name — two Swoops, two Wild Acts — cash them in on your turn: discard both and take a Prop Token. Venue is irrelevant; each card type appears four times in the deck, once per venue, so a pair is a name match.

👀 Every held card sits face-up, so the card you need is visible in front of whoever holds it. That is what makes Pie in the Face, Stage Hook and Stage Left Stage Right worth aiming rather than firing at random.

⚡ Using Powers

Every Power Card in your hand has a printed power. Play it to trigger its effect, then discard the card — powers are single-use. The sentence printed on the card is the whole power; there is no separate bonus for playing your own animal's card.

🃏 When the Deck Runs Out

Reshuffle the discard pile into a new deck and keep going.

🎉 Winning

The moment a player reaches their table's token target — 3 at 3–5 players, 2 at 6–8 — the game ends immediately and that player takes a bow.

⚡ When the Pool runs dry

Someone has earned a Prop Token but there are none left in the Pool. The table picks one of two things:

  • Open another cracker — one more Prop Token goes into the Pool and play carries on as normal.
  • Declare a substitute — grab any object to hand: a coin, a cork, a bottle top. The next player to earn a Prop Token by any route takes the substitute and wins immediately, no matter what anyone's tally says.
⚡ Sudden death is a real ending, not a tiebreak. Once a substitute is on the table the game can finish on the very next turn — including for a player who is well behind.
