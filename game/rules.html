How to Play

Everything you need to run a full game at the table — or let the Companion App run it for you.

🎲 Let the App Guide You

🏆 The Goal

Be the first player to collect 2 Prop Tokens.

🎭 The Four Venues

Every Power Card and every Character belongs to one venue. The venue tells you what kind of performance is required.

VenueWhat you do
🎤 The Comedy ClubTell jokes — setup, punchline, deadpan delivery
🎵 The RSLSing or rhyme — lyrics, rhythm, improvised verse
🎪 The Royal ShowClown around — mime, physical stunts, slapstick
🎭 The School PlayAct — accents, emotions, roleplay, impressions

🧩 Setup

Pick the number of players (3–8). Each player chooses a Character (which fixes their animal and home venue) and a Prompt Card (1–16, their personal script). Player 1 starts.

🔁 Your Turn

1Draw. Draw the top Power Card from the deck, face-up for everyone to see. The card shows a venue, an animal, and a printed power.

2Perform. The card's venue tells you which kind of performance to give. Use the next unused prompt for that venue from your Prompt Card. Prompts are used in order — no going back, no cherry-picking.

Your prompt tells you how you'll be judged:

IconMeaning
🗳️ VoteMajority of the table decides. Tie goes against you.
✅ ObjectiveThe table can see it happen or not. No vote needed.
👤 JudgeThe player to your left decides alone.

3The Verdict.

Fail → the card goes to the discard pile. Turn ends.

Succeed → your reward is resolved in this order:

  1. 🐾 Animal Affinity — Does the animal on the card match your Character's animal? If yes, take a Prop Token. Turn ends.
  2. ✅ Venue Match — Does the card's venue match your Character's venue? If yes, keep the card face-up in your hand. Once you hold two matching cards, cash them for a Prop Token. Turn ends.
  3. 🃏 No Match — Keep the card face-up in your hand for its printed power. Turn ends.
💡 Playing digitally? The Companion App auto-detects the outcome from the card data — no manual scoring needed.

🔁 Cashing a Pair

If you hold two Power Cards that both match your Character's venue, cash them in as a free action: discard both cards and take a Prop Token.

⚡ Using Powers

Every Power Card in your hand has a printed power. Play it to trigger its effect, then discard the card. Many cards also have an Animal Affinity bonus — an extra effect if the matching animal plays it.

🃏 When the Deck Runs Out

Reshuffle the discard pile into a new deck and keep going.

🎉 Winning

The moment a player reaches their 2nd Prop Token, the game ends and that player takes a bow.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Mode

Removes the Heckler and Mime Time cards from the deck and hides adult-themed prompts — for a gentler, mixed-age table.
