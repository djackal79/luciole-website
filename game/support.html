FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many players do I need?

Pull Another One is built for 3–8 players. It plays fastest and loudest with 5 or 6, but the app and rules scale cleanly across the whole range.

What ages is it suitable for?

The base game includes a few cheeky, adult-leaning prompts. Turn on Family Mode — in the rules or in the Companion App — to remove the Heckler and Mime Time cards and hide adult-themed prompts for a gentler, mixed-age table.

Do I need the Companion App to play?

No — the physical game is fully playable with the printed cards and the rules on our How to Play page. The free Digital Companion App just takes the bookkeeping off your hands: it tracks the deck, hands, tokens, and automatically resolves Animal Affinity and Venue Match calls.

What if the app makes the wrong call?

On the verdict screen, tap "Wrong call? Change…" to manually override the outcome — the table's ruling always wins.

Can we play across multiple devices?

Yes. The Companion App supports real-time multiplayer, so each player can join from their own phone, or you can pass a single device around the table (pass-and-play).

Is there a way to learn the game before playing?

Turn on Tutorial Mode when starting a game in the Companion App. It walks new players through the rules, characters, and basic strategy step by step.

When can I buy Pull Another One?

The game is in final testing and not yet available for purchase. Drop your email on the homepage or email us directly and we'll let you know the moment it's ready.

I found a bug in the Companion App — where do I report it?

Email us with your device type, browser, and what happened, and we'll take a look. See contact details below.
